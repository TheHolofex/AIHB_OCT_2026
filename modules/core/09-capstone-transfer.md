## Why

Saved artifacts are the claim. A method is finished only when it works on material the learner has not seen, survives a challenge and a restart without conversational memory, and carries enough evidence for someone else to operate it without being coached.

## Enabling objectives

1. Assemble only the components the work earns, and preserve standards under unfamiliar work and one adverse change.
2. Make a package usable in a fresh session and by another person without relying on the author's chat history.
3. Use recipient observations to resolve gaps in operating, stopping, restoring, and handing off the package.

## Check the work

A clean session receives only the RUNNABLE_PACKAGE, ordinary operating access, and the supplied task. Inspect its run, stop, and restore behavior. Separately, give another person the same package and task. Preserve what they do, the questions they ask, and any help supplied. PO09_RESULT records those observations and unresolved limits so the package can be improved. A technical replay and a person-to-person attempt remain separate evidence; neither is a learner score.
