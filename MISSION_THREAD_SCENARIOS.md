## Eight steps are the only logistics model

The eight steps are the handoff model. They are not one shared operation. Monday afternoon's clock, 14:05 MDT on 6 October 2026, belongs only to that packet. Other sessions use the movement named for them above, and their own clock. They do not reuse Monday afternoon's calculated results or proper names.

1. Requirement defined — destination, usable quantity, route, deadline.
2. Cargo received — warehouse records the exact totes and lots in custody.
3. Cargo released — quality office identifies which lots may be used.
4. Vehicle made ready — released load plus required rack fit the vehicle.
5. Movement authorized — permit applies to the exact vehicle and route.
6. Route window met — vehicle can reach the gate before it closes.
7. Cargo delivered — route can reach the clinic by the deadline; later evidence records actual delivery.
8. Usable effect confirmed — clinic records receipt of the required released quantity.

Expected arrival is not delivery. Delivery is not clinic confirmation. A warehouse receipt is not a quality release. Intake of a permit is not approval.

## Reopen a claim on these seven questions

| Part | Question |
|---|---|
| Identity | Exact mission, route, vehicle, permit, lot, clinic, and source revision? |
| Authority | Is this source allowed to establish this kind of fact? |
| Time | Current at the decision time, and is the zone understood? |
| Quantity or condition | Count, units, required equipment, and state correct? |
| Dependency | What had to be true before this step could begin? |
| Handoff | Does this step's output meet the next step's entry condition? |
| Uncertainty | Unknown, assumed, contradicted, or not yet observed? |

Label a material statement `SOURCE FACT`, `CALCULATION`, `INFERENCE`, `DECISION`, or `UNSUPPORTED`. Split a mixed sentence until each row has one label. These labels are Module 01's tool. Later sessions use the distinctions; they do not reteach the ledger.
