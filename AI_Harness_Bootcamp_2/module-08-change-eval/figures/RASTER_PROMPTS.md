## m08-fan-out

- Title: Ask twice. Do not share the answer.
- Claims and sources split to a source reviewer and a skeptical reviewer. Each has its own session. The other review, the notes, and the exact findings are not sent.

## m08-one-writer

- Title: One agent writes the file.
- Source review, skeptical review, and exact checks feed one correcting agent. A review is not an edit. null means unknown. Agreement does not choose the value.

## m08-fresh-review

- Title: Check the rewrite without the old answers.
- Corrected claims and original sources go to two new sessions. First verdicts and writer notes are not sent.
