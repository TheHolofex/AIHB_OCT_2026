# Module 1 · Verify sources and outputs

Challenge the polished Cold Lantern brief against the applicable sources and your own calculations. Decide whether to accept, revise, reject, or hold it for internal class review. A **mission thread** connects each step in the movement to what the next step needs.

Plan for about three hours. That is a rough estimate, not a measured time.

The packet has every domain rule you need. First check whether each record covers the right vehicle, route, cargo, and time. Then ask what the record can prove and whether that is enough for the next step.

## Start here

Use the bounded direction and source-checking habits you've already practiced. For every claim that could change the decision, find the applicable record, redo any calculation, and explain whether the result supports the next step. Make the final verdict yourself.

## The brief under review

The fictional Cold Lantern brief says 216 medicine kits can leave Red Mesa Depot on truck VX-204, use Route R-71, and reach Clinic H-17 by 16:00.

Parts of the brief sound right, but the result also depends on:

- which lots are usable rather than merely scanned;
- whether required refrigeration equipment counts toward payload;
- whether the current gate time is UTC or local;
- whether a permit was approved or only received for processing;
- whether cited documents concern VX-204 and R-71; and
- whether an expected arrival is being mistaken for completed delivery.


## What counts as evidence

Open each source yourself. Record its identity and version, quote the relevant text with a locator, and explain whether it supports the claim and why. Show the supported premises and units for your calculations.

The AI system's confidence, citation list, and self-review do not count as independent evidence.

## Class-only boundary

All names, routes, documents, quantities, and organizations here are fictional course fixtures. Don't use this packet to plan, authorize, dispatch, or describe a real movement. A module result is for class review only.
